CEMENT & BUILDING MATERIALS IN KENYA: A PRACTICAL BUYING GUIDE

Choosing construction materials starts with understanding the requirements of the project, the product specification and the quantity needed. This guide gives customers a simple starting point when comparing cement and selected building materials in Kenya.

Choosing the right cement

Start with the cement grade and the requirements of your project rather than choosing on price alone. For technical or structural applications, follow the specification provided by your engineer, architect, contractor or project documents.

Our shop currently lists Simba Cement 32.5R and Simba Power 42.5N. Visit the individual product pages for the listed price and product description.

Buying paving blocks

For paving work, consider the area to be covered, the required pattern and the installation method before ordering. Our 600mm paving blocks page provides the current listed product information.

Planning fencing materials

For boundary fencing, measure the length of the fence and determine the height required before selecting materials. The website lists barbed wire in 240m, 480m and 610m lengths, plus chainlink fencing in 4ft, 5ft, 6ft and 7ft by 18m options.

Before you place an order

  • Confirm the product specification and quantity.
  • Check the latest price and stock before payment.
  • Provide the delivery location when requesting a quotation.
  • For technical construction decisions, follow your project's professional specifications.
