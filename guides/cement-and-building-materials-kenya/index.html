SIMBA CEMENT KENYA: CEMENT & BUILDING MATERIALS BUYING GUIDE

Choosing construction materials starts with understanding the requirements of the project, the product specification and the quantity needed. This guide gives customers in Kenya a practical starting point when comparing cement and selected building materials from Simba Cement Kenya.

Choosing the right Simba Cement

Start with the cement grade and the requirements of your project rather than choosing on price alone. For technical or structural applications, follow the specification provided by your engineer, architect, contractor or project documents.

Buying paving blocks
