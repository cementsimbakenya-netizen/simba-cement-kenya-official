ABOUT SIMBA CEMENT KENYA

BUILDING KENYA,
ONE PROJECT AT A TIME.

Simba Cement is built around dependable construction materials for homes, businesses and infrastructure projects across Kenya. Production and supply under the Simba Cement brand began in 2010, supporting customers with locally available cement for everyday building needs.

As demand grew, National Cement Company Limited expanded grinding capacity fivefold in 2013. The business also developed local clinker production at Merueshi, Kenya, strengthening the local supply chain and reducing reliance on imported core raw materials.

Today, our focus is straightforward: dependable products, practical service and a convenient way for customers to place orders and speak directly with our sales team.
